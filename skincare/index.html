About the Doctor

Dr. Priyanka – Consultant Dermatologist

Dr. Priyanka is a highly experienced dermatologist with over 12 years of clinical expertise in diagnosing and treating a wide range of skin, hair, and nail conditions. Throughout her career, she has successfully treated more than 10,000 patients, earning a reputation for providing personalized and evidence-based dermatological care.

She completed her MBBS from MediCiti Institute of Medical Sciences and subsequently pursued her DDVL (Diploma in Dermatology, Venereology & Leprosy) from Chalmeda Anand Rao Institute of Medical Sciences.

Qualifications & Professional Registration

  • MBBS (2005–2011)
  • DDVL (2012–2014)
  • Medical Registration No.: 72729

She secured the highest percentage of marks in dermatology in her year of passing across combined Telangana and Andhra Pradesh. From childhood, she has received several academic and professional awards.

Deeply service-oriented, she has organized and participated in dermatology awareness and treatment camps in her hometown and in Manikonda, bringing skin care support to the wider community.

Dr. Priyanka has been recognized for her contributions to dermatology and was featured on the front page of Femina, reflecting her growing influence and expertise in the field.

With a patient-centric approach and a commitment to delivering high-quality skincare solutions, Dr. Priyanka specializes in the treatment of acne, pigmentation disorders, hair loss, skin allergies, cosmetic dermatology procedures, and overall skin health management.

Healthy skin is the foundation of confidence, and Dr. Priyanka is dedicated to helping every patient achieve it through expert care and modern dermatological treatments.
