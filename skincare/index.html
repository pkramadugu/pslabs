Patient journey

Simple steps, thoughtful supervision.

The goal is not to sell procedures. It is to understand what your skin needs and choose the least complicated route to visible, stable progress.

  1. Consult

    Discuss concerns, triggers, goals, medical history, and current products.

  2. Plan

    Receive a treatment and home-care plan matched to your skin type and timeline.

  3. Treat

    Begin procedures, prescriptions, or routine corrections with clear care instructions.

  4. Review

    Track progress and adjust the plan as your skin responds.
